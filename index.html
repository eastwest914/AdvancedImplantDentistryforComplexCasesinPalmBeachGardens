Questions About Advanced Implant Care

What counts as a complex implant case?

Examples include significant bone loss, multiple missing teeth, previous implant failure or a bite that needs rebuilding. We review each case individually.


What are my permanent tooth replacement options?

Options can include single implants, implant bridges, implant-supported dentures and full arch solutions. The best choice depends on how many teeth are missing and the condition of your bone.


Can a failed implant be fixed?

Often there are options. We examine the implant and surrounding tissue, then discuss whether treatment, revision or replacement makes sense.


Do you offer cosmetic treatment alongside implants?

Yes. We can plan a smile makeover that combines cosmetic and restorative work for a more natural look.
